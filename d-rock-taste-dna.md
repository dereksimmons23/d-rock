## The Foo Fighters Poem

Starts with wide type and narrows down back, centered. Tattoo concept.

Beneath the Everlong glow of February Stars I would Come Alive

Once & for All, I Am a River; the Sky, a Neighborhood
Stacked Actor in a White Limo ain't My Hero

In Times Like These
