# D-Rock as Lyricist in the Studio

**Date captured:** April 1-3, 2026 (Empire Builder)
**Status:** Seed — direction for D-Rock project

From the train notes: "D-Rock is not in the studio, and I think he should be. Lyricist. Liner notes. If I ever paint, I'm signing it Deek. Coach D needs D's voice, not Sonnet's. Claude Will. could be a signature for digital and probably should be if it's trademarked. Derek Simmons is not trademarked. The goal should be anonymity."

The idea: D-Rock (the voice-only AI DJ, early stage per the ecosystem) should function as a lyricist — generating song lyrics and liner notes as part of the creative process, not just as a DJ. Liner notes as a form are underrated: they're the essay version of a song, the place where the story behind the music lives.

Separate thread: Derek's pen name/alias system:
- D-Rock = the DJ, the voice project
- Deek = painting signature (given, not invented — grandmother Vernie: "you can't nickname yourself")
- Coach D = coaching persona
- Claude Will. = digital signature (trademark filed March 29)
- Derek Simmons = not trademarked, the private life he wants to protect

The anonymity principle: each persona is intentional, not random or cute. They were given, not chosen. D-Rock in the studio producing lyrics is a distinct voice from Sonnet writing marketing copy — it needs its own register, its own aesthetic.

Cross-project connection: The train notes on the song fragments (lines 1-250) are exactly the kind of raw material D-Rock as lyricist should be working with. The song "Until It Ain't" is Derek's voice. D-Rock could generate variant verses, liner note drafts, B-side material.
